How to read this page
  • Points are Crooked scoring.
  • ± is how much the sources disagree, not game-to-game luck; START/UPGRADE odds include game-to-game swing, each player's own from his projected stat mix (the small floor–ceiling under his points: one game in five below, one in five above), or the position's typical spread (σout K 5.0, DEF 5.7).
  • Tiers group players statistically indistinguishable from each tier's best.
  • Kickers: no form is added to a kicker's number. His range, when one is shown, comes from how many field goals and extra points he is projected for; without one, his odds use the typical kicker spread above.
  • Kicker moves: the league sets no limit on kickers, so only roster room counts. With a free roster spot you just add; with a full roster you drop your other kicker, or swap the new one for your starter.
  • Team defenses: no form and no range of their own; their odds use the typical defense spread above. Points allowed score the tier that the projected points allowed fall in.
  • Defense moves: the league sets no limit on defenses, so only roster room counts. With a free roster spot you just add; with a full roster you drop your other defense (with no other playable one, one of yours that can't play this week, usually on bye), or swap the new one for your starter.
  • Matched: the share of a source's players with an NFL team that were matched to a Sleeper player (unmatched ones are listed, never dropped silently).
  • * Projection first captured after kickoff, so it can't be proven pre-game; Sleeper's projections haven't been seen to change once games start.
  • Scores are Crooked points from Sleeper's stats: "so far" while a game is on, final 4 hours after kickoff.
