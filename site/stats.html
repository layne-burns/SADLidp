What these mean
  • Avg miss: the average gap between our number and what the player scored. Lower is better.
  • Simple guess: the player's own average so far this season (players with 2+ games). If we don't beat it, the projections add nothing.
  • Runs high/low: whether our numbers are too high or too low on average.
  • Our number: the μ the page showed at a player's last sync before his game (the frozen record), the number every suggestion and tier used.
  • Ranks players right: how well the order of our numbers matches the order of the scores within a week (0 = no better than random, 1 = perfect), averaged over the weeks, ± its margin of error. Shown from 4 weeks on.
  • Below / inside / above the range: how often the score landed below the card's floor, between floor and ceiling, or above the ceiling. About 20 / 60 / 20 means the ranges are honest. IDP scores have long tails, so the old "68% within one swing" target doesn't apply.
  • Spread check: the typical miss divided by the stated spread. 1.00 means the odds are as sure as they should be.
  • Decision: one week, one position and one of your players that suggestions were measured against. Several badges over the same player count once.
  • ± (margin of error): about a 95% range, from how much the number moves week to week. "Can't tell" means the difference is inside it.
  • Typical miss (RMSE): like the average miss, but big misses count extra.
  • Top-12 hits: of our 12 best-projected, how many finished in the actual top 12 at the position (ties at 12th count). "Random" is what 12 picked at random from the 50 would land: about 12 × (how many of the actual top 12 were among the 50) ÷ 50. For K, with about 28 kickers playing a week: our 5 best, the actual top 5 and a pool of 20.
  • Odds score (Brier): how good our win probabilities are. A coin flip scores 0.250; lower is better.
